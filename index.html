A few things to know

First hackathon?
You’re in.

Do I need a team or an idea already?

Teams of 2–4 are the sample format. You can bring an idea or arrive curious and find inspiration at the kickoff.

Who can take part?

This sample event is for currently enrolled students at the organizing college. Check the final event announcement for eligibility.

What should we bring?

Bring your student ID, laptop, charger, and anything you need to build. The organizers will confirm venue access, meals, and equipment.

Is the registration fee final?

No. ₹999 per team is an assumed planning figure, not an official payment request. Wait for confirmed college instructions before paying.
